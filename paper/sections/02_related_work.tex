\paragraph{Multi-resolution terrain and seams.}
Geometry clipmaps~\cite{losasso2004}, ROAM~\cite{duchaineau1997} and geomipmapping~\cite{deboer2000} treat the join between a coarse and a fine mesh as a central engineering problem, and \citet{pajarola2007} survey the family. Work that continues this line still addresses transitions between levels~\cite{zhang2019,jain2024}. This literature optimises how a transition looks. Our RQ1 measures what a physics query returns at the join, which is the quantity a landing leg meets.

\paragraph{Compact and infinite support.}
Gaussian-type radial functions~\cite{hardy1971} never reach exactly zero at a finite radius, while compactly supported radial functions of minimal degree~\cite{wendland1995} and multi-scale compactly supported bases~\cite{ohtake2003,ohtake2003mpu} do. These results explain why the shape of a fade function can matter once a surface is truncated at a finite radius, and they supply the candidate fades compared in RQ1.

\paragraph{Terrain accuracy in lunar landing.}
Precise-landing programmes such as ALHAT rely on terrain-relative navigation and hazard detection~\cite{johnson2008,nasajpl}, and lunar elevation models quantify the terrain accuracy available at landing sites~\cite{barker2016}. Recent flight data from vision-based navigation and obstacle detection on SLIM~\cite{ishida2025} show that terrain representation reaches landing outcomes in flight, which motivates asking the same question of a simulator.

\paragraph{Simulation platforms and evaluation practice.}
Isaac Lab and its predecessor Orbit define the platform used here~\cite{mittal2023,nvidia2025}. Terrain curricula and randomisation are established practice~\cite{rudin2022,tobin2017}, and OmniLRS builds lunar scenes in Isaac Sim from a real elevation model plus procedural detail~\cite{richard2024}. Work on evaluation practice in reinforcement learning stresses repeated runs, interval estimates and stated statistical power~\cite{henderson2018,agarwal2021,colas2018,colas2019}. We adopt the same discipline for a simulator study: paired seeds, bootstrap intervals, an A/A noise floor, and a stated minimum detectable effect.
